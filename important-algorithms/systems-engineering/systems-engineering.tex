\chapter{Algorithms for Systems Engineering}
\label{ch:systems-algorithms}

Systems software---runtimes, kernels, network stacks---leans on a small set of algorithms that rarely appear in algorithms courses. They are shaped less by asymptotic elegance than by the machine: fixed-size words, cache lines, locks, and the cost of a system call. This chapter collects them, each with its invariant, its operations, and what they cost.
